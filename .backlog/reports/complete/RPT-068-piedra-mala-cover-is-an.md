## Details

The photo selection rule asks for daylight photos showing water and sand. This beach has one picture, with `pictureQualityScore` 1.

## Where found

- "Playas cercanas" on `/playas/playa-de-el-rincon`

## Evidence

- The red arrow is visible on the card at 1280px and 375px.
- 30 of the 140 beaches with pictures have a score of 1 or lower, so others may need review.
