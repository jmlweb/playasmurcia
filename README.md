# Playas de Murcia - Your Ultimate Guide to Costa Cálida

A comprehensive digital platform dedicated to showcasing the stunning coastline of the Region of Murcia, Spain. This project serves as a detailed travel guide for tourists and locals looking to explore the Mediterranean shores and the unique Mar Menor.

🌐 **Visit the website:** [https://www.playasmurcia.com/](https://www.playasmurcia.com/)

---

## 🏖️ Project Overview

**Playas de Murcia** aims to be the most reliable source of information for beachgoers in the Region of Murcia. The website provides a curated directory of beaches, coves, and coastal spots, helping users discover the diverse beauty of the "Costa Cálida."

### What You Will Find
* **Complete Beach Directory:** Detailed profiles of beaches in Águilas, Lorca, Mazarrón, Cartagena, La Manga, San Javier, and San Pedro del Pinatar.
* **Essential Information:** Details on sand type, wave conditions, accessibility features, and blue flag status.
* **Available Services:** Information on parking, security, toilets, and nearby restaurants or "chiringuitos."
* **Natural Wonders:** Guides to protected areas, hidden coves, and the unique landscape of the Mar Menor.
* **Local Recommendations:** Travel tips and must-visit spots to make the most of your Mediterranean experience.

---

## 🗺️ Areas Covered

The guide organizes the Murcian coastline into its main municipalities and geographical areas:

* **Mar Menor:** Europe's largest saltwater lagoon, famous for its calm and warm waters.
* **Cartagena & La Manga:** A mix of historical coastlines and the iconic sandy strip.
* **Mazarrón & Lorca:** Home to dramatic cliffs and pristine natural beaches.
* **Águilas:** The southernmost point with crystal clear waters and beautiful urban bays.
* **San Pedro del Pinatar & San Javier:** Salt marshes and family-friendly shores.
